About our school

Family-run, Pietermaritzburg-based, since 1990

Delta Driving School was founded in 1990 by Dawn Bekker. Today it's led by her daughter Linda, who learned the craft directly from her mother and now carries the same passion for road safety into every lesson.

Backed by decades of experience and a genuine love of teaching, our award-winning team is dedicated to turning anxious beginners into safe, confident drivers — whether you're stepping behind the wheel for the first time or upgrading a professional licence.

High-quality instruction shouldn't break the bank. With 37+ years of proven excellence and modern, patient teaching methods, Delta delivers some of the highest pass rates in the region.

  • Certified instructors with years of experience
  • Modern, well-maintained training vehicles
  • Day, evening & weekend lessons
  • Competitive pricing, no hidden fees
  • High first-time pass rate
  • Free pickup and drop-off, every lesson

How it works

Your road to a licence

Four stages, one instructor from start to finish. We handle the scheduling, the test-day paperwork and the practice runs.

1

Learner's licence

Our K53 workshop covers the rules of the road and road signs, Mondays and Tuesdays.

2

Behind the wheel

Structured lessons in the code you need, with free pickup and drop-off every time.

3

Test day

Vehicle usage, final practice and paperwork sorted — you just need to arrive calm.

4

Licensed driver

You leave with a licence and the road sense to actually use it, for life.

Inside a lesson

Every plan explained, before you get behind the wheel

Each session starts with a few minutes going through exactly what you'll practise and why — so by the time the engine's running, you already know what to expect.
